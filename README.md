# From Femslash to Lesbian

Replication data and figures for Laura Untner and Anastasia Glawion, *From Femslash to Lesbian: Marking Female
Same-Sex Desire on AO3, with Sappho as a Case Study* (DH 2027, Glasgow).

- `replication data/` – SPARQL queries and results, derived data, analysis and figure scripts (see its README)
- `figures/` – Figures 1–16 (PNG, 300 dpi, and SVG)

The raw AO3 metadata and the full texts of the Sappho corpus are not included.
